Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026. This page describes corporate, non-personal data handling only.

What we collect

Global Market Intel Scraper collects corporate, non-personal information only: publicly published business news, press releases, government notices and trade-journal articles, together with their publication dates, sources and subjects. We do not collect, store or sell personal data, and the dashboard requires no account, sign-in or profile.

What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, locations, device identifiers, payment details or browsing histories tied to an individual. Filter choices, the data-saver toggle and export actions run in the visitor's own browser and are not sent to us. Articles on this site link to their original publishers and contain no personal data.

Cookies and advertising

This site itself sets no tracking cookies. If advertising is added later through an approved network, that network may set its own cookies under its own policy, and this page will be updated to name it before any such unit goes live. No advertising units are live on this site yet; if advertising is added later, this page will be updated to name the network before any unit goes live.

Sources and copyright

Articles are summarised and linked in a way that respects publishers: headlines and short summaries point readers to the original source, and full text is not republished without permission. Publishers may request removal or correction of a listing via the contact page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact page.
