About Global Market Intel Scraper

Global Market Intel Scraper is a software-as-a-service market utility for corporate readers who follow trade across borders. It turns scattered foreign-language publishing — corporate press pages, regional trade journals, government portals and port bulletins — into one searchable, filterable English dashboard.

Crawl

FreshRSS in Docker manages feeds and XPath scraping; a Newspaper4k cron job extracts clean article text without sidebars, ads or navigation.

Translate

Self-hosted LibreTranslate (50+ languages) and Meta's NLLB-200 model convert raw text to clean English, with a glossary for corporate and legal terms.

Deliver

Astro builds fast, indexable static pages from the structured database, filterable by sector, sentiment and region, exportable as CSV or JSON.

The platform serves logistics managers, procurement leads, agritech buyers, commodity traders and compliance officers. It is a reading and reporting utility, not a broker, customs agent or legal adviser — and it always shows the source, date and original wording beside every translation.
